SAFETY & PARTICIPATION

Readiness comes before participation.

Enlightenment Week uses an application, conversation, and screening process because not every experience is appropriate for every person.

Not medical treatment

Enlightenment Week is not represented as medical care, psychotherapy, diagnosis, or treatment for any disease or condition. Nothing on this website replaces advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

Screening is required

An initial application is only the first step. Prospective participants may be asked for additional health, medication, and history information through a separate screening process. Acceptance may be declined or withdrawn when participation is not considered appropriate.

Medication and health considerations

Some health conditions, medications, supplements, or recent experiences may be incompatible with program activities. Never stop or change prescribed medication based on website content or program communications. Discuss potential participation with your prescribing clinician.

Informed participation

Selected applicants receive information about the planned activities, expectations, preparation, and known considerations before enrollment. Participation in any optional activity requires informed agreement and compliance with the program’s safety guidance.

No guaranteed result

Individual experiences vary. Emotional, spiritual, relational, or personal-development outcomes cannot be predicted or guaranteed.

Preparation and integration

Participants are expected to complete preparation guidance, follow on-site agreements, communicate concerns promptly, and engage responsibly with integration after returning home.

Urgent needs

This site does not provide crisis or emergency support. If you believe you may harm yourself or another person, or you are experiencing a medical emergency, contact local emergency services immediately.
