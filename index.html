01 — WHY NOW

Nothing has to fall apart
before you choose to pay attention.

Your life may hold every visible sign of progress. People trust you, depend on you, and look to you for answers. Yet the skills that built this chapter may also keep you moving too quickly to hear what the next one requires.

Enlightenment Week is seven days in Costa Rica for a small, selected group: time away from the roles and routines that reinforce familiar answers, with preparation before arrival and integration designed for the life you return to.

More productivity will not answer a question of meaning.

Another achievement will not tell you what “enough” feels like.

The next chapter cannot be heard at the volume of the current one.

03 — THE METHOD

THE RETURN SEQUENCE

The work follows a deliberate sequence.

Preparation begins before the flight. On site, each day holds 8–14 hours of assignments, facilitated activities, group sessions, reflection, and integration. After departure, weekly group integration continues for 12 months.

01

Disconnect

Leave the roles, notifications, and familiar rooms that keep familiar answers in place.

02

Descend

Slow down enough to encounter the question beneath the question—the one achievement has not answered.

03

Discern

Separate what is true now from what was inherited, performed, postponed, or outgrown.

04

Design the return

Turn what becomes clear into decisions, conversations, boundaries, and a plan that can survive Monday morning.

Guided journaling gives the week’s insights language—so they return with you as decisions, conversations, and a plan.

04 — THE SEVEN-DAY ARC

SUNDAY ARRIVAL · SATURDAY COMPLETION

Seven days, each with a clear intention.

Before01

Prepare

Complete 5–10 hours of private orientation, intention-setting, journaling, readiness review, and practical preparation.

Sunday02

Arrive

Check in, meet the small circle, review agreements, receive the weekly schedule, and establish intentions together.

Monday03

Release

Use guided reflection, journaling, forgiveness work, group process, and the Tree of Life to begin loosening what has been carried.

Tuesday04

Transform

Examine core beliefs and self-perception through the Quantum Collapse Process, reflection, and facilitated group work.

Wednesday05

Reorient

Turn toward inner alignment through writing, guided exercises, quiet attention, and the week’s unfolding process.

Thursday06

Purpose

Explore purpose and the future you want to build, with an extended evening session held in a protected group setting.

Friday07

Integrate

Spend time in silence, meditation, and practical reflection so that insight becomes language, decisions, and a return plan.

Saturday08

Complete

Close the week with shared reflection, gratitude, departure orientation, and entry into the year-long integration community.

After09

Continue

Join weekly group integration for 12 months and carry the work into relationships, leadership, and ordinary life.

The sequence above reflects the program framework in the original invitation. Exact activities, timing, location, and facilitator plan are confirmed to selected applicants before enrollment.

THE STANDARD

This is not for everyone.
That is part of the design.

You may be ready if…

  • You want honest reflection, not spectacle
  • You can follow preparation and group agreements
  • You are willing to discuss readiness candidly
  • You understand that no result can be guaranteed
  • You want insight that can withstand ordinary life

This is not the right next step if…

  • You want an unsupervised experience
  • You are seeking urgent medical or mental-health care
  • You expect a cure or promised transformation
  • You are unwilling to complete screening
  • You cannot follow participation guidance
