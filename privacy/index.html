PRIVACY POLICY · LAST UPDATED AUGUST 11, 2026

Your information should be treated with care.

This policy explains how Kingdom Of Elohim Corporation collects, uses, and protects information submitted through Enlightenment Week.

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including your name, email address, telephone number, location, application responses, communications, and other information you choose to share. If you proceed in the selection process, additional screening or enrollment information may be requested separately.

How we use information

We use information to provide requested materials, respond to inquiries, review applications, assess program fit, communicate about Enlightenment Week, operate and improve the website, maintain security, and meet legal obligations. We do not promise acceptance based on information submitted.

Sensitive information

Application or screening responses may include sensitive personal information. Please provide only what is requested. This website’s initial application is not an emergency, medical, or therapeutic service. More detailed screening, when applicable, is handled separately.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be shared with personnel and service providers who support website hosting, communications, application review, security, or program administration. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a legitimate business reorganization. We do not sell personal information for money.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, including legal, safety, and recordkeeping needs. We use reasonable safeguards, but no website or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of information, or opt out of promotional communications, subject to legal and operational retention requirements. Privacy rights may vary by location.

Cookies and analytics

The site may use essential technologies and analytics to operate, understand usage, prevent abuse, and improve performance. If advertising or remarketing technologies are added, this policy and any required consent choices should be updated before use.

Children

Enlightenment Week is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly solicit applications from minors.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Kingdom Of Elohim Corporation at (813) 445-5578.
